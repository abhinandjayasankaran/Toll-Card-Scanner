Toll Card Scanner

Point the camera at a card. It is found, cropped, straightened and sent to your Mac by itself, then you place the next card.

Connecting to your Mac…
  • Dark, plain surface under the card gives the fastest, most reliable edge detection.
  • Fill most of the frame with the card and avoid glare on the number.
  • Swap cards when you see “Next card”. Duplicates are skipped automatically.

Tip: tap Share → Add to Home Screen to install this as an app.

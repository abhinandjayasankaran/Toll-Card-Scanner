Connecting to the scanner on your Mac…

One-time phone setup

Your iPhone only allows the camera on a secure connection. Trust your Mac’s scanner certificate once (about a minute) and it will connect automatically from then on.

  1. Download the certificate
    Tap the button, then Allow and Close. Download certificate
  2. Install it
    Open Settings → tap Profile Downloaded near the top (or General → VPN & Device Management) → Install.
  3. Turn on full trust
    Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings → switch on Toll Card Scanner Local CA.
  4. Come back here
    This page continues by itself, or tap the button.
Open without installing (shows a warning)

Skipping works in Safari after tapping Show Details → visit this website, but installing the scanner on the Home Screen needs the certificate. Phone and Mac must be on the same Wi-Fi.
